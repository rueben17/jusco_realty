Principal agent

Justin Amenya

I founded Jusco Realtors to work the way I'd want an agent to work for me: a few homes at a time, honest pricing advice, and one person who answers the phone. I handle sales and lettings of family homes and apartments across Nairobi's western and northern suburbs.

Buyers from abroad can view by video call first. I coordinate land searches, valuers and advocates so the paperwork moves at the same pace as the deal.

How I work
01

The brief

We meet, in person or on a call, to agree budget, neighbourhoods, schools and timing. Sellers get a written price opinion based on recent comparable sales.

02

Viewings

I shortlist homes against the brief, including some not advertised publicly, and accompany every viewing myself.

03

Offer to keys

I negotiate the offer, then coordinate the land search, valuation and both advocates through to completion and handover.
